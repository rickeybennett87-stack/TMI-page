Program Overview

The Infrastructure of Restoration

The certification content exists and is free. What the market cannot provide — and what your support funds — is everything else.

Week 1

Workplace Readiness

Every participant begins with IBM Workplace Readiness training — professional communication, time management, and job search strategy. Every participant earns a recognized IBM badge in their first week.

Throughout

Cohort Accountability

Weekly check-ins, peer accountability partners, and Institute navigation support. No participant figures out the coursework alone. The cohort is how the mandate becomes community.

End of Program

Direct Placement Support

Resume development, LinkedIn profile creation, mock interview preparation, and direct employer network introductions. The Institute's obligation is not fulfilled until the participant has a job.

Always

Faith & Spiritual Support

Economic displacement is not only a practical crisis — it is a spiritual one. Prayer, scripture, and a community that understands the theological weight of being removed from your vocation.
