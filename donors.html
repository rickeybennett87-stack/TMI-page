The Case for Giving

What Your Gift Does

TMI does not deliver the certification content — that content is free and already exists. What TMI provides is everything else: the case management, the cohort structure, the accountability, the job placement support, the faith community, and the device access for participants who arrive without equipment.

None of that is free. Every gift funds the infrastructure of restoration — the human labor of walking alongside someone through a process that free platforms cannot support alone.

◆

Cohort Operations

Weekly check-ins, accountability partnerships, peer community, and the coordination infrastructure that keeps participants moving through their pathways to completion.

◇

Case Management

Individual navigation support, pathway selection, digital literacy assessment, and the ongoing relationship that free platforms cannot replicate.

✦

Job Placement

Resume building, LinkedIn setup, mock interviews, and direct employer introductions. We do not stop until the participant is employed.

□

Device Access

Participants who arrive without a computer cannot complete an online certification. Gifts fund device access and connectivity support for those who need it most.
