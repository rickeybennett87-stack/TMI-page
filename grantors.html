Who We Are

Mission Statement

The Mandate

The Thessalonian Mandate Institute exists to equip technologically displaced workers with the skills, credentials, community, and support necessary to return to dignified, sustainable employment. We operate from the conviction that the work mandate of scripture carries with it a corresponding community obligation: when technology displaces a willing worker, the community of faith is obligated to equip.

All services are provided at no cost to participants. Our job does not end until the participant has a job.

Program Descriptions

Programs & Services

TMI operates seven certification pathways and one wraparound services program. All pathways are delivered in cohorts of 8–15 participants. Every participant receives the wraparound services described in Program 8 regardless of which certification pathway they pursue.

Program 1  ·  Foundational

Digital Literacy Foundations

Foundational computer, internet, and device proficiency training for participants with limited prior technology access. This program serves as the prerequisite pathway for all technical certification tracks and ensures that no participant is turned away due to baseline skill gaps. Delivered in partnership with GCFGlobal and Khan Academy.

Duration2–4 weeks
Cost to ParticipantFree
CredentialGCFGlobal Completion Certificate  ·  Khan Academy Skill Badges
Program 2  ·  Administrative Technology

Microsoft Office & Administrative Technology

Foundational office technology training preparing participants for administrative, clerical, and office support employment across all sectors. Covers Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook in addition to general workplace technology proficiency. Broadly applicable across all educational and professional backgrounds.

Duration4–8 weeks
Cost to ParticipantFree
CredentialMicrosoft Office Specialist Certification  ·  GCFGlobal Certificate
Target RolesAdministrative Assistant, Office Coordinator, Data Entry Specialist
Program 3  ·  Information Technology

IT Support & Helpdesk

Entry-level information technology support training providing participants with the skills and credentials required for helpdesk and IT support roles. This pathway offers the fastest entry point into technology employment and requires no prior technical experience. Delivered through the Google IT Support Professional Certificate and IBM IT Support Badge pathways.

Duration8–12 weeks
Cost to ParticipantFree
CredentialGoogle IT Support Certificate  ·  IBM IT Support Badge
Starting Salary Range$35,000–$55,000
Program 4  ·  Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Foundational cybersecurity training addressing one of the highest-demand and most severely understaffed fields in technology. Requires no prior IT experience and prepares participants for entry-level security operations, incident response, and compliance roles. Delivered through the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate and IBM Cybersecurity Fundamentals Badge.

Duration10–14 weeks
Cost to ParticipantFree
CredentialGoogle Cybersecurity Certificate  ·  IBM Cybersecurity Fundamentals Badge
Starting Salary Range$50,000–$70,000
Program 5  ·  Cloud Computing

Cloud Computing Basics

Cloud infrastructure fundamentals training preparing participants for roles across the Microsoft Azure ecosystem and the broader cloud sector. Cloud skills are required across virtually every technology role. The Institute pursues Microsoft for Nonprofits voucher coverage for the AZ-900 examination for all participants.

Duration8–10 weeks
Cost to ParticipantFree (exam voucher pursued via nonprofit program)
CredentialMicrosoft Azure Fundamentals AZ-900  ·  IBM Cloud Essentials Badge
Target RolesCloud Operations Technician, Systems Administrator
Program 6  ·  Data Analytics

Data Analytics

Data literacy and analytics training providing participants with the most broadly applicable technical competency in the modern economy. Entry-level data roles exist across every industry sector and require no prior technical experience. Delivered through the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate and IBM Data Analyst Badge.

Duration12–16 weeks
Cost to ParticipantFree
CredentialGoogle Data Analytics Professional Certificate  ·  IBM Data Analyst Badge
Starting Salary Range$45,000–$65,000
Program 7  ·  AI & Automation

AI Tools & Workflow Automation

Practical artificial intelligence literacy and workflow automation training designed specifically for workers displaced by AI. Teaches participants to work effectively with AI tools rather than be replaced by them — a universally applicable competency across every industry and role type. The most immediately relevant pathway for the AI-displaced worker.

Duration4–6 weeks
Cost to ParticipantFree
CredentialIBM AI Fundamentals Badge  ·  Anthropic AI Fluency Certificate
Target RolesAI Operations Associate, Workflow Automation Coordinator
Program 8  ·  Wraparound Services

Cohort Support & Job Placement Services

The infrastructure that transforms free online certification into actual employment. TMI’s core service offering is not the certification content — that content already exists and is free. TMI provides the case management, cohort accountability, faith and emotional support, pathway navigation, and direct job placement support that free platforms cannot replicate. Every participant receives: intake assessment, cohort orientation, weekly accountability check-ins, peer community, device access coordination where needed, resume development, LinkedIn profile creation, mock interview preparation, and direct employer network introductions.

DurationConcurrent with all pathways through employment
Cohort Size8–15 participants
Outcome TargetEmployment within 90 days of certification
Population Served

Who We Serve

TMI serves adults who have been displaced from their vocations by automation, artificial intelligence, or technological obsolescence — with particular attention to those navigating compounding barriers including long-term unemployment, housing instability, and limited prior technology access.

The Institute was founded by a minister who navigated twenty-three years of homelessness and labor market exclusion while preaching the dignity of work. The population we serve is not abstract. It is the founder’s own community.
