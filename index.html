The Founding

The Mandate

For eleven years, Rickey Jay Bennett II preached the dignity of labor on the streets of America. Not from a pulpit. Without credentials or institutional backing — only a Bible and a conviction that work is sacred. That the capacity for productive, dignified labor is not a feature of economic life but a feature of human nature, given by a Creator who worked in the beginning.

She preached this from the exact position of someone the economy had left behind. For twenty-three years she navigated homelessness, instability, and a labor market that strips a willing worker of every foothold. She understood from the inside what it means to be willing, to be able, to be trying — and still to be without.

The Thessalonian Mandate Institute is what she built when she stopped waiting for someone else to build it. It was founded on the conviction that the work mandate of scripture is not a comfort. It is a command — and a command carries with it a corresponding obligation. When technology displaces a worker from their vocation, the community of faith is obligated to equip.

The mandate stands. Now we build.

How We Walk With You

We Are Not a School. We Are a Community.

TMI does not deliver curriculum — it delivers people to curriculum that already exists, wraps that journey in community and theological purpose, and provides the case management, accountability, and job placement support that free platforms cannot. Our job does not end until you have a job.

The Cohort Model

You Will Not Do This Alone

Human beings were not created to learn alone. All pathways are delivered in cohorts of 8–15 participants. Community accountability is one of the most powerful drivers of completion in adult workforce training. This is a theological conviction, not a logistical preference.

Week 0

Intake & Assessment

Pathway selection, digital literacy baseline, and device access coordination for participants without equipment.

Week 1

Cohort Orientation

All participants complete IBM Workplace Readiness together, building cohort identity and earning a digital credential immediately.

Weeks 2–12

Primary Pathway

Individual pathway training with weekly cohort check-ins, peer support, and Institute navigation assistance.

Final 2 Weeks

Portfolio & Placement

Resume building, LinkedIn profile, mock interviews, and direct employer network introductions. Graduation and credential presentation.

Post-Graduation

Alumni Network

Graduates mentor incoming cohorts, fulfilling the theological obligation of the equipped to equip others.
